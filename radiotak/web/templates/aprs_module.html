Policy

Callsigns show up under Units as system APRS (CoT UID RADIOTAK-APRS-{CALL}). With Auto-approve on, heard stations get Forward to TAK immediately and use the Settings default stale time (unit stale = 0). Otherwise enable Forward to TAK per unit before positions or GeoChat leave the gateway. Disable a unit under Units to hard-block it even when auto-approve is on. Decode/receive does not require a license; transmitting APRS or IGating does (GMRS does not cover APRS TX). TX / digipeat / IGate are out of scope for this module.
